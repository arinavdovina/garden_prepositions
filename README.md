# Pocket Garden

An English garden game with eight plants, four growth stages, 24 questions on **in / on / at**, and a final garden riddle using prepositions of place.

## Open the game

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation, account, internet connection or build step is needed. Keep the asset folder alongside the HTML, CSS and JavaScript files. Progress lasts until the page is reloaded.

## Play

1. Choose each plant and tap an empty patch, or drag it into place.
2. After all eight are planted, the first question opens. Choose **Water**, then hover over or tap a plant. Each correct answer advances one stage. Incorrect answers can be retried. Fully grown plants add an item to the harvest box.
3. Choose **Move plants** to rearrange the garden. Tap a plant and then another patch; occupied patches swap. Desktop dragging also works.
4. Enter the final round whenever you like. If some plants have not been planted, they are placed automatically. They keep their current growth stages.
5. Follow the eight clues and choose **Check my garden**. You can return to growing at any time.

On a phone, swipe the garden sideways to reach all patches. Buttons support keyboard navigation with Tab and Enter; Escape closes a question.

## GitHub Pages

Upload the contents of this folder to a repository, keeping `index.html` at the root. In the repository's Pages settings, publish the branch and folder containing `index.html`. No framework or package installation is required.

## Teacher key

Viewed left to right:

| Row | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Back | Sunflower | Tomato | Peas | Pepper |
| Front | Corn | Strawberry | Cucumber | Aubergine |

Behind / in front of means the same column. Next to means an immediately adjacent patch in the same row. The riddle has one solution. Plant names, grammar help and per-clue correctness hints are not shown in the game. The language uses British English (aubergine).

## Artwork

Uses the supplied garden illustration, 32 growth-stage illustrations, eight harvest images, watering can and crate. White backgrounds have been removed from growth-stage images, retaining white flowers; duplicate supplied files are omitted. Images are compressed as WebP. No external fonts, libraries or analytics are loaded.
